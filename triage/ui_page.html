Lead Triage Review

How to use this page
  • Decisions: every lead with the agent's decision, track and scores. Filter, sort by any column (value, highest first, by default), and tick Show rationale to see why: the score breakdown, the model's quotes from the lead, the injection-screen verdict and the message as submitted.
  • Review: the work a person does. First the escalations: each card says the question to answer and what each answer leads to. Then the bad-faith batch list: auto-rejected leads to deny or allow by sender, and the current deny and allow lists.
  • Source data: the input CSV exactly as submitted, beside each decision, to check the triage against the source.
  • Tune: change the weights and thresholds and see what moves: decisions, scores and the order of the sales queue. Hover or tap ⓘ for what each setting does. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Click any column heading to sort; click again to reverse. Sorted by value, highest first, to start.

Escalations: leads a person must decide

Each card states the question to answer and what each answer leads to, computed by re-running the agent's rules with that answer applied.

Secondary escalation: bad-faith batch review

Auto-rejected as bad faith; lower urgency, reviewed in batches. Deny rejects this sender on every future submission; allow vouches for them (bad-faith checks skipped, phishing never). Both write to known_actors in rules.json and re-decide every lead.

Deny and allow lists

The senders a person has already judged. Removing an entry re-decides every lead.

Every row of the input CSV exactly as submitted, beside the agent's decision, so the triage can be checked against the source. Click a heading to sort.

Change any weight or threshold. Every lead is re-decided by the agent's own policy on the cached model outputs; the Effect panel shows what moved against the saved rules. Value weights mostly change scores and the sales-queue order; thresholds and budget rules change decisions. Nothing is saved until you press Save.
